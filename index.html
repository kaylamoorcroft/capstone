Computer Science options

There are multiple pathways for Computer Science at Acadia University

  • Bachelor of Computer Science (BCS)
  • Bachelor of Computer Science (BCS) with Honours
  • Bachelor of Applied Computer Science (BACS)
    • BACS with Mobile and Ubiquitous Computing Option
    • BACS with Game Development Defined Option
    • BACS with Software Development Defined Option
    • Data Analytics Option (Special Program)
    • BACS with Interdisciplinary Study

All these pathways have core common classes and then additional specific requirements. Make sure to complete the questionnaire to set up your profile.
